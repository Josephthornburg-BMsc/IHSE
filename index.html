Class I Hominid

Sasquatch

Gigantopithecus canadensis (Hypothetical)

Primary Habitat Pacific Northwest, North America
Average Height 7.5 - 9.0 ft (2.3 - 2.7 m)

Bipedal primate characterized by a sagittal crest, heavy musculature, and specialized dermal friction ridge structures on the feet. Field evidence includes massive plaster castings and low-frequency acoustic vocal signatures.

Class III Aerial

Mothman

Anomalopteryx wv-rubra (Hypothetical)

Primary Habitat Point Pleasant, West Virginia
Wing Span 8.0 - 10.0 ft (2.4 - 3.0 m)

Winged hominid exhibiting high-intensity bioluminescent ocular cells. Observations detail high-speed vertical takeoffs, structural meditation behaviors, and low acoustic warning sounds prior to electromagnetic disturbances.

Class II Marine

Nessie

Nessiteras rhombopteryx (Hypothetical)

Primary Habitat Loch Ness, Scotland
Body Length 30.0 - 45.0 ft (9.1 - 13.7 m)

Aquatic reptile structurally similar to relic plesiosaurs. Features four rhomboidal flippers, a elongated neck structure, and specialized acoustic echoing signatures detected via lake bottom sonar arrays.

Class V Carnivore

Chupacabra

Canis vampyrus (Hypothetical)

Primary Habitat Puerto Rico / Southwest USA
Mass Index 35 - 55 lbs (15.8 - 25.0 kg)

Predatory quad-pedal canine hybrid, notable for dorsal spikes, specialized fangs, and hematophagous feeding patterns. eDNA profiles from saliva swabbing reveal complex hybrid canid-lupine genomes.
