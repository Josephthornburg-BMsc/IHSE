Scientific Exploration

The Study of Hominology

Applying scientific rigor to relict hominoid studies globally.

Hominology occupies the intersection of paleoanthropology, zoology, and field exploration. The Institute of Hominology & Scientific Exploration (IHSE) was established to investigate testimonies, biological artifacts, bioacoustic recordings, and geographic footprint trackways that suggest the presence of unclassified, relict hominoid species in the remote wilderness corridors of North America and Eurasia.

We reject sensationalism. Our approach relies on rigorous field methodologies, biometric footprint documentation, and modern laboratory testing of hair, skin, and eDNA samples. By establishing this webstore, we allow enthusiasts, academics, and recruits to obtain precise castings of physical evidence and scientific literature, funding active field work in the process.

High-Fidelity Casts

Our footprint and bone casts are produced using strict archaeological replication procedures. Molds are sourced directly from authenticated field prints and cast using extra-strength dental gypsum to prevent shrinkage and detail degradation.

Bioacoustic Studies

The IHSE maintains high-sensitivity audio recorders in deep-wilderness locations. By purchasing our recommended bioacoustic arrays, you deploy the exact equipment our researchers trust for recording anomalous wood knocks and vocalizations.

Your support directly enables our research teams to conduct systematic, multi-week tracking expeditions. All catalog sales contribute to the field expenses, satellite communication feeds, and local packing guides required to explore safely and bring back concrete evidence.
